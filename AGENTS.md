# Publishing destination

This repository is the canonical source for Jacob Chin's public blog at `https://jacobchin.github.io`.

- Add new articles to `_posts/` and publish them by pushing to `main` in `JacobChin/jacobchin.github.io`.
- Article permalinks belong under `https://jacobchin.github.io/articles/...`.
- Do not publish blog articles to the separate OpenAI Sites / `chatgpt.site` project.
- Preserve the existing article structure and design unless Jacob requests a redesign.
